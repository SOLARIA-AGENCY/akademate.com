# Akademate Public Web V2 - Optimized Product and Design Prompt

## Objective

Rebuild the public Akademate website as a premium international SaaS experience for an Academy Operating System. The site must explain how Akademate connects audience growth, public websites, course discovery, reservations, enrolment, payments, academic operations, virtual learning, people, finance and insight across in-person, online and hybrid academies.

Akademate is not positioned primarily as an AI product. AI-assisted workflows are an optional capability inside a broader operational platform.

## Product narrative

The central journey is:

`Create the offer -> publish the page -> attract demand -> register or reserve -> collect payment -> deliver learning -> operate the academy -> understand performance`

The public site must make the following distribution model unmistakable:

1. Every customer can launch a complete branded academy website on an Akademate subdomain.
2. Customers can connect their own domain through guided DNS configuration.
3. Customers that keep an existing website can embed Akademate modules for classes, course discovery, registration, payment, forms and availability.
4. Every course, workshop, camp, cohort or event can receive its own shareable page with a short URL, social preview, schedule, instructor, capacity, attendees, reviews, registration, social login and payment journey.
5. Links must be ready to share through Instagram, Facebook, messaging, email and advertising campaigns.

## Product modules to communicate

- Public website, pages, blog, SEO, navigation, custom domains and reusable embeds.
- Course, workshop, event and programme pages with reservations, applications, waitlists, capacity and checkout.
- CRM, lead capture, Meta Ads, attribution, follow-up, conversion and automation.
- Courses, cohorts, sessions, schedules, rooms, campuses, equipment and attendance.
- Students, guardians, teachers, coaches, administrators, teams, roles and permissions.
- Virtual campus, lessons, resources, assignments, quizzes, grades, feedback, progress, certificates and community.
- Email, notifications, internal chat, announcements, reminders and support workflows.
- Payments, deposits, instalments, subscriptions, memberships, SEPA, Stripe, PayPal, invoices, refunds and reconciliation.
- Finance and accounting: receivables, payables, ledger, cost centres, bank reconciliation, accounting exports and finance APIs.
- HR and workforce: contracts, availability, workload, time tracking, payroll inputs, leave and performance context.
- Library and inventory: lending, catalogues, equipment, rooms, facilities, stock and asset responsibility.
- Reporting, dashboards, governed exports, API, webhooks, integrations and optional MCP tools.
- AI-assisted summaries, content drafts, next actions and analytics with permission-aware human review.
- Privacy, governance, audit and security controls without implying certifications.

## Visual thesis

Premium operational clarity with subtle editorial energy. Use Inter throughout, deep ink-blue surfaces, one electric-blue accent, thin tonal dividers, restrained shadows and a consistent soft geometry. Replace anonymous lifestyle imagery in the hero with product-led visuals that show the real Akademate workflow.

## System rules

- Inter is the only public marketing typeface. Remove monospace labels and repetitive uppercase eyebrows.
- Use one shape system: 16px content surfaces, 12px controls, pill buttons and tags.
- Use one page theme family with cool white, mist blue and ink navy; no random section inversions.
- Prefer large product surfaces, editorial grids, dividers and whitespace over generic card mosaics.
- Give every page and major section its own visual purpose.
- Use real interactive previews for forms, checkout and course pages. Do not use decorative fake dashboards.
- Use generated raster assets only where they add product narrative, atmosphere or social-sharing value.

## Home information architecture

1. Full-viewport hero with concise Academy Operating System promise and a four-moment product carousel.
2. Proof and customer context.
3. Publish anywhere: subdomain, custom domain, full site or embedded modules.
4. Shareable course/workshop page preview with social login, attendees, rating, capacity, registration and payment.
5. Connected journey from campaign to enrolment and delivery.
6. Academy workspaces: growth, operations, teaching, people, finance and insight.
7. Virtual campus and teacher/student experience.
8. Complete platform breadth and planned operational modules.
9. Customer verticals.
10. Plans and final conversion.

## Interaction thesis

- Hero product carousel advances through four coherent product surfaces and remains keyboard accessible.
- Product sections use restrained scroll depth/parallax on imagery and reveal only when it improves the narrative.
- Interactive previews change registration options and selected platform modes without pretending to submit real data.
- All motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion` and degrades to a stable static layout.

## Claim boundaries

- Use confident SaaS marketing language, but do not describe placeholders as live integrations.
- Present future modules through roadmap language where appropriate.
- Do not use official regulatory logos or imply certification.
- Do not identify fictional academies as real customers.
- Keep CEP-specific identity, data, contracts and endpoints outside the public SaaS site.

## Quality gate

- Desktop and mobile navigation, typography and first viewport verified.
- No monospace marketing copy or repetitive all-caps eyebrows.
- Consistent radii, colors, button labels and CTA intent.
- Interactive previews accessible by keyboard with labeled controls.
- Typecheck, tests and build pass.
- Visual QA at desktop and mobile sizes.
- Console and network checked locally.
- Commit, push and deploy remain separately reported.
