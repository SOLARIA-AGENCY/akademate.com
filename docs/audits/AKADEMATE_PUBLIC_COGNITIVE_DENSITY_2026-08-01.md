# Akademate public cognitive-density audit

Date: 2026-08-01
Scope: public `akademate.com` commercial routes only. CEP is excluded.
Preset: `landing-page`, verified at 390 px and 1440 px.

## Visual thesis

Akademate should read as an academy operating system at a glance: one idea per section, one dominant visual, short supporting copy and repeatable feature anatomy.

## Baseline

- 16 commercial routes inspected on mobile and desktop.
- 111 mobile paragraphs exceeded two rendered lines.
- Home contained 28 paragraph violations.
- Equivalent module cards mixed prose and dot-separated capability strings.
- Heading scale created 29 additional three-line mobile blocks.

## Solution Registry

| Approach                                                          | Correctness | Maintainability | Scalability | Simplicity | Pragmatism |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------: | --------------: | ----------: | ---------: | ---------: |
| CSS truncation only                                               |           5 |               5 |           6 |          9 |          7 |
| Central copy taxonomy, symmetric lists and defensive visual limit |           9 |               9 |           9 |          8 |          9 |

Selected: central copy taxonomy plus symmetric lists. CSS is only a defensive boundary; source copy was also reduced.

## Implemented contract

- Commercial paragraph, card and CTA copy renders in no more than two lines.
- Mobile `h1`, `h2` and `h3` use a restrained type scale and also remain within two lines.
- Legal documents and long-form article bodies are excluded from the compact-copy contract.
- Every platform pillar exposes exactly five list items.
- Repeated cards use the same title, summary and list anatomy.
- Home, Features, Pricing, customer types, Blog index, Company, Contact and Courses share the contract.
- Product descriptions remain centralised in marketing registries.

## Result

- 0 paragraph violations across the 16-route mobile and desktop audit.
- 0 heading violations across the 16-route mobile and desktop audit.
- Product module lists are symmetric and individually scannable.
- Learner experience image regenerated with one person, two anatomically coherent arms and two hands.
- Four publication modes now have distinct product previews rather than one repeated template.
- Course sharing exposes a browser URL, desktop/mobile share flows, capacity and confirmed attendees.
- Pricing includes per-plan checklists and a responsive five-area comparison matrix.
- Every connector pillar contains at least eight locally served brand marks, with roadmap state kept explicit.

## Evidence boundary

The audit covers rendered line count, responsive overflow, asset loading, interaction regression and visual inspection. It does not claim that two lines is universally optimal for long-form editorial or legal content; those surfaces intentionally preserve complete prose.
