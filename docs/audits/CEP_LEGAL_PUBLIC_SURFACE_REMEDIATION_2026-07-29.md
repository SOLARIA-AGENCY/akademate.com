# CEP public legal surface remediation — 2026-07-29

## Scope

Local implementation for the public legal surface served under `/p/legal/*` in
`apps/tenant-admin`. This record does not prove deployment, legal approval,
GDPR certification, AI Act conformity assessment, or production operation.

## Implemented

- Central legal hub at `/p/legal`.
- Claim-safe visual identifiers `RGPD` and `AI ACT` linking to the relevant
  information, with an explicit non-certification notice.
- One shared content authority for the public and authenticated privacy, terms
  and cookies routes.
- CEP identified as controller for its academic purposes and SOLARIA AGENCY OÜ
  as technology processor under the applicable contract.
- Privacy information corrected for rights, identity verification, response
  window, retention criteria, transfers, security evidence and automated
  decision-making.
- Terms corrected to avoid unverified role access, generic refund percentages,
  consumer-forum waivers and unsupported service guarantees.
- Cookie inventory aligned with the consent manager: optional GA4, GTM and Meta
  remain disabled until the relevant consent; Plausible and fictional
  preference cookies are no longer stated as active.
- AI transparency expanded for human oversight, information/labelling,
  AI literacy, provider inventory and disabled high-risk educational uses.
